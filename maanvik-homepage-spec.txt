DESIGN SYSTEM
=============
Overall style:
- Modern industrial
- Premium B2B
- Minimal
- Clean
- Trustworthy
- Product-focused

Avoid:
- Generic corporate templates
- Excessive gradients
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Huge rounded cards everywhere
- Excessive animations
- Fake statistics
- Fake testimonials
- Fake certifications
- Fake client logos
- Unverified claims

Suggested base palette:
- Background: #F7F7F5
- Primary text: #111111
- Secondary text: #5F6368
- Borders: #E5E5E2
- Accent: choose a strong professional blue after logo exploration

Typography:
- Use Geist, Inter, or Manrope
- Large editorial-style headings
- Strong contrast between headings and body text
- Comfortable line height
- Generous whitespace

Visual language:
- Real product photography wherever available
- Close-up label/barcode imagery
- Clean product cutouts
- Subtle barcode/label-inspired graphic details
- Thin borders
- Structured grids
- Strong alignment

ANIMATION
=========
Use subtle, purposeful motion:
- Fade/slide section reveals
- Product card hover states
- Smooth navbar transition
- Subtle image movement
- Button hover transitions

Do not over-animate the page.

RESPONSIVE REQUIREMENTS
=======================
Desktop:
- Spacious editorial layout
- Large hero
- Multi-column product grids
- Sticky navbar

Tablet:
- Reduce typography and spacing
- 2-column product grids

Mobile:
- Excellent typography and spacing
- Hamburger navigation
- Full-width CTAs
- 1-column product cards where appropriate
- No horizontal overflow
- Keep contact and WhatsApp actions easy to access

================================
Framework:

- Tailwind CSS
- App Router

Recommended:
